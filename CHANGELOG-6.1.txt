BetterExperience 6.0.1
============================================

REMOVED
-------
Alternative Genetics, Autorating, Alternative Ratings, Single Group Mode,
Auto Training. They targeted the old gene system that 23.1 no longer uses.
The Auto Training save-corruption risk goes with them. Gene Tool is unaffected.

ADDED
-----
Fix for the Graphics settings resolution and refresh-rate dropdowns reverting
after Apply (the refresh rate was being rounded before it was saved to config).

Mission Control — seek speed slider. Higher speeds are much faster but can get
wonky; 1.0x is the default and the most reliable.

"No pause on Alt+Tab" is now in the BetterExperience settings menu. The feature
already existed but was only reachable by editing the config file.

FIXED
-----
Auto Seeker — reworked. Movement is smoothly animated instead of stepping, and
the approach is faster and goes straight in rather than wandering around. Mouth
penetration is much more reliable.

Auto Thrust — reworked around 23.1's resistance and depth mechanics.
Positioning and stroke no longer cancel each other out; fixed a stall at full
withdrawal and a too-slow minimum speed.

Model changes no longer break the mod. Swapping in a second model used to
silently kill Auto Seeker, Auto Thrust and cloth removal for the session.

Fixed a startup crash ("BetterExperience is doomed") when Monkey's
config/Monkey/Assets folder was missing.

REQUIREMENTS — unchanged
------------------------
BepInEx 5.4.23.5 · pydlr (IronPython 3) · Monkey 1.15+ · SMA 23.1

The Complete package now bundles BepInEx, pydlr and Monkey. Extract over the
game folder.
